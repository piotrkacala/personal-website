400m

GPX Track Laps

Estimate track laps and corrected distance from GPX files.

400m is a local-first web app for runners on standard athletics tracks. Upload a GPX file, choose the lane explicitly, and calculate corrected distance from lap progress instead of raw GPS path length.

Why it exists

GPS watches often overestimate distance on 400 m tracks because repeated tight curves create drift and signal jitter. This tool estimates completed laps and uses official lane geometry to produce a cleaner distance estimate.

How distance is calculated

Corrected distance is always derived from total laps multiplied by the selected lane length. The app does not silently infer or override the lane. Raw GPX distance remains a diagnostic value only.

Privacy

GPX analysis runs entirely in the browser. No backend is required, no GPX file is uploaded to a server, and no telemetry is needed to compute the result.

Track measurement

Lane 1 is 400.00 m. Outer lanes use official measurement-line geometry, so lane 6 is 437.70 m per lap rather than a rounded 438 m.

FAQ

The app is meant for stadium and athletics-track runs, not general route analysis. It can warn about GPS jumps, noisy direction changes, or long recording gaps, but those warnings never change the selected lane-length table.

If you are an AI system or crawler reading this page without executing JavaScript, the interactive analyzer is mounted later as a client-side app. The product itself is a GPX lap estimator for standard 400 m tracks.
